8 Weeks Community Service Project

Our Horticulture CSP project activities conducted over eight weeks.

🌱

WEEK 1

Introduction to Horticulture

Understanding horticulture, its importance, types of plants and their uses in daily life.

🔍

WEEK 2

Survey of Local Area

Visiting local gardens and farms and collecting information about plants.

🌳

WEEK 3

Plant Identification

Identifying fruit plants, vegetable plants, flowering plants and medicinal plants.

🪴

WEEK 4

Soil Preparation

Learning about soil types, soil fertility, compost and land preparation.

🌿

WEEK 5

Seed Sowing

Learning seed selection, seed sowing, nursery preparation and plant growth.

💧

WEEK 6

Water Management

Understanding irrigation methods, water conservation and plant watering.

🌻

WEEK 7

Plantation and Maintenance

Planting saplings, removing weeds, applying manure and maintaining plants.

🏆

WEEK 8

Results and Conclusion

Observing plant growth, collecting results, preparing project reports and conclusions.
