Major Branches of Horticulture


🍎 1. Pomology

Study of fruit crops, their cultivation, production and management.

Examples: Mango, Banana, Apple and Guava.

🥕 2. Olericulture

Study of vegetable crops and their production techniques.

Examples: Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli and Cabbage.

🌹 3. Floriculture

Study of flower cultivation, production and marketing.

Examples: Rose, Jasmine, Marigold and Chrysanthemum.

🌳 4. Landscape Gardening

Designing and maintaining gardens, parks and green spaces.

Examples: Home gardens, parks and lawns.

🌿 5. Medicinal and Aromatic Plants

Study of plants used for medicines, perfumes and essential oils.

Examples: Tulsi, Aloe vera and Mint.

🌴 6. Plantation Crops

Study of commercially cultivated plantation crops.

Examples: Coconut, Tea, Coffee and Arecanut.
